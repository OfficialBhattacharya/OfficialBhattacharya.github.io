ML from Scratch

Machine learning built from first principles, with pen-and-paper maths, small from-scratch code and live figures: decision trees and random forests, bagging, boosting and XGBoost, then neural networks from the perceptron through backpropagation to modern training.

Started November 15, 2026By Diganta BhattacharyaInteractive figures in every lecture
  1. Decision Trees (CART) and Random ForestsRecursive yes/no questions, the impurity measures that choose them, why a single deep tree overfits, and how averaging many randomised trees fixes it.
  2. Bagging vs Boosting, and XGBoost from the Objective UpTwo opposite ways to combine weak learners: average independent ones, or build them in sequence to fix each other's mistakes. Gradient boosting as gradient descent, and the second-order maths inside XGBoost.
  3. Neural Networks I: From the Perceptron to the Multilayer NetworkA neuron is a weighted sum and a squashing function. One of them can only draw a straight line; stack them with a non-linearity in between and they can draw anything.
  4. Neural Networks II: Backpropagation from ScratchThe chain rule, organised so that one backward sweep gives every gradient. Worked by hand on a tiny network, then vectorised.
  5. Neural Networks III: Training That Actually WorksLosses, mini-batches, momentum and Adam, sensible initialisation, normalisation and regularisation. The practical toolkit that turns backprop into a model that generalises.
