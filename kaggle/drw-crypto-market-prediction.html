What we tried

The task: predict the next-minute return of a crypto asset from order-book statistics and about 800 anonymised market features, scored by Pearson correlation.

The first week went on cheap one-shot baselines (LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost, ElasticNet, plus a chunked trainer for memory) to see which model family coped with the wide, noisy feature set. The main idea after that was market regimes: detect regimes (a hidden Markov model in the pre-processing notebook), then train regime-wise, time-decayed XGBoost that weights recent rows more. That grew into an XGBoost + LightGBM + regimes + engineered-features notebook (19 versions), an end-to-end notebook, and a final blend called iBlend.

None of the 20 runs received a leaderboard score: every one ended with a submission error. The modelling work is written up in the two notebooks linked below.
