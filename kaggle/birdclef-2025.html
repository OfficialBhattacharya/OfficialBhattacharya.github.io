What we tried

Identify which of 206 species call in each 5-second window of rainforest recordings, scored by macro ROC-AUC.

The early one-shot analysis submissions scored 0.50 (blank predictions, used to test the pipeline). Then came an EfficientNet-B0 spectrogram classifier (0.81), a focal-loss + BCE baseline (0.83), and finally a power adjustment for low-ranked classes post-processing step. That step lifted the score to 0.851 public, 0.873 private.
