What we tried

Classify body-focused repetitive behaviours (such as hair pulling or skin picking) from a wrist device with an IMU, thermopiles and time-of-flight sensors.

A hand-built IMU + thermal + ToF feature baseline scored 0.63 to 0.66. Moving to sequence models made the big jump: a model-switching approach (IMU-only vs full sensors, depending on what's available) reached 0.81, and a TensorFlow blending model over all three sensor streams reached 0.820 public, 0.811 private.
