What we tried

Jane Street's anonymised market data, served one time step at a time through an inference API, scored by a sample-weighted zero-mean R². Scores are tiny for everyone, and anything above zero beats predicting nothing.

The progression went from a Ridge baseline (raw, then scaled, then with clipped predictions), through ElasticNet and LightGBM (including a symbol-wise variant, which over-fitted to −0.015), to an ensembler. It ended with a neural net + XGBoost blend without clipping, which reached about 0.008. Kaggle re-ran that notebook on fresh data during the forecasting phase, which is why the same model has five scores between February and May 2025.
