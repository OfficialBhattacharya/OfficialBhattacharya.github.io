What we tried

Extract exoplanet atmospheric spectra from simulated Ariel telescope data, scored by a Gaussian log-likelihood that rewards honest uncertainty.

Two approaches went head to head. A quantile-regression ML strategy scored 0.117 public and 0.145 private. A non-ML transit-curve fit (modelling the dip in starlight directly) scored 0.250 and 0.264, more than double. Here a good physical model beat a generic learner.
