What we tried

Predict calories burned from age, sex, height, weight, heart rate, body temperature and workout duration, scored by RMSLE.

This was the most-iterated playground. It started from a regression template with XGBoost and CatBoost (about 0.0575), then moved to cohort models by age and sex and lots of feature and target-transform experiments, ending with a tuned solo XGBoost. A few experiments that skipped the log transform scored far worse (above 1.0), which shows how much the transform matters for RMSLE. The best result was 0.05661 public, 0.05859 private.
