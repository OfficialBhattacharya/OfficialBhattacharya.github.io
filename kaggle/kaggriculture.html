What we tried

A two-player, turn-based farming game: plant, tend, harvest and sell against a rival, rated on a skill ladder by wins and losses.

v1 Herd & Wheat was a greedy job scheduler with a crew sized to the workload (456). v2 Melon & Herd ranked actions by value (465), v3c Dawn Warehouse sold off excess stock before the end-of-day price drop (538), and v3b Sale Race moved sell orders to the earliest market slots. The final agent, First in Line: Stock Into Income, built on public community agents (Apache-2.0, with attribution kept), reached 1,305 on the public board and 1,538 on the ladder.
