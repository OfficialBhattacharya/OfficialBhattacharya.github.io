What we tried

Predict five physical properties of a polymer (glass transition, density, radius of gyration and others) from its SMILES string, scored by a weighted MAE.

Everything was built on RDKit molecular descriptors feeding gradient-boosted trees. The first versions had a validation bug (CV=0). After fixing it, the XGBoost starter went through more than 20 versions, adding external polymer datasets to the sparse targets, followed by a LightGBM variant and finally a multi-model notebook that trains a separate model per property. The best public score was 0.0744, and the best private score was 0.0966.
