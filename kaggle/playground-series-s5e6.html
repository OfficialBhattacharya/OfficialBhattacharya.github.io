What we tried

Multi-class: recommend the right fertilizer from soil, crop and weather, scored by MAP@3, so the model has to rank its top three classes well.

The work started with an exhaustive EDA, then a 5-model baseline with an ensemble (0.31 to 0.34) and an XGBoost tuner (0.349). The final submission blended four strong public solutions with my own models, reaching 0.3809 public, 0.3821 private.
