Highlights & Attractions

Ha Long Bay

Nearly 2,000 limestone islands and islets rising from emerald water, a UNESCO site best seen from a junk cruise at dawn.

Hanoi Old Quarter

The thousand-year-old capital: 36 guild streets, Hoan Kiem Lake, the train street and endless bowls of pho.

Hoi An Ancient Town

A preserved trading port of yellow shophouses, the Japanese Covered Bridge and silk lanterns on the Thu Bon river.

Hue Imperial City

The walled citadel of the Nguyen emperors, with royal tombs along the Perfume River.

Mekong Delta

Floating markets, coconut-candy workshops and a maze of canals through Vietnam's rice bowl.

Sapa

Hill-tribe villages and rice terraces carved into the slopes below Fansipan, the roof of Indochina.

Cu Chi Tunnels

Over 120 km of hand-dug wartime tunnels outside Ho Chi Minh City.

Phong Nha caves

Some of the world's largest caves, including Son Doong, in a national park of jungle and karst.

Vietnam Culture & Heritage

Street Food

Pho, banh mi, bun cha and banh xeo, eaten on low plastic stools on the pavement, is the soul of the country.

Coffee Culture

Strong drip coffee with condensed milk, and Hanoi's famous egg coffee whipped like a dessert.

Ao Dai

The elegant national dress: a long silk tunic worn over trousers, seen at schools, weddings and Tet.

Water Puppetry

A thousand-year-old art from the northern rice paddies, performed on a pool of water to live music.

Tet

Lunar New Year, the biggest festival: family reunions, peach blossoms, banh chung and lucky money.

Crafts

Lacquerware, silk lanterns, conical non la hats and ceramic villages like Bat Trang.
