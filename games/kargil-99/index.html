KARGIL '99

May–July 1999. Pakistani intruders hold the heights above NH1A. India has seven turns to hold, or retake, Kargil.

A historical simulation with abstract unit values, made for fun and learning. Its mechanics follow the classic introductory hex wargame Battle for Moscow (Frank Chadwick, GDW); the map, units and scenario here are new. Online play connects the two browsers directly through PeerJS's free public broker.
